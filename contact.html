Planning a project

To put a proposal together we need to know:

  • What covers the ground: grass, plowed field, woods, pavement or water?
  • How large is the area?
  • What are the local soils and geology?
  • Has there been earlier archaeological work there?
  • What question do you need answered?

Please email or call to talk it through.
