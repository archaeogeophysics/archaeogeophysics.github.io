Archaeo-Geophysical Associates

Archaeo-Geophysical Associates, LLC (AGA) is an archaeological consulting firm that specializes in archaeological geophysics: mapping what lies below the ground before digging.

We work with cultural resource management firms, government agencies, tribes, universities and cemetery boards, on research projects and compliance work alike. Our results help decide where to dig, and where not to.

Magnetometry

Fast mapping of whole sites and landscapes with ground carts and drones, including drone magnetometry for maritime projects. More about magnetometry

Ground-penetrating radar

Detailed maps at different depths of walls, floors, pits and graves. More about GPR

Drone lidar

Bare-earth models of mounds, earthworks and landscapes, even under trees. More about drone lidar

Cemeteries

Searches for unmarked graves, with a clear statement of what the data can and cannot show. More about cemetery investigations
