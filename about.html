About AGA

Chet Walker, Ph.D.

Chet Walker founded Archaeo-Geophysical Associates, LLC in the spring of 2006. He is an archaeologist who specializes in archaeological geophysics and GIS, with nearly 30 years of experience in archaeology.

His research looks for new ways to use archaeological geophysics and mapping data as primary archaeological evidence, to answer archaeological questions directly. His interests include the archaeology of Native America (North, Central and South America), World War II battlefield archaeology, and military recovery (MIA) work.

  • Ph.D. in Anthropology, University of Texas at Austin, 2009
  • M.A. in Anthropology, University of Memphis, 2000
  • B.A. in Anthropology, Texas State University, 1997
  • Research Affiliate, Center for Regional Heritage Research, Stephen F. Austin State University (2014 to present)

History

AGA has been in business since 2006 and online since 2007. Chet's publications and technical reports cover research in 15 countries and 30 US states and territories, for universities, cultural resource management firms and non-profit organizations, at sites ranging from the early prehistoric to the historic period.
