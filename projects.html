Published projects

A selection of AGA projects that have been published. Many more are in client reports that are not public.

Etowah, Georgia

A long-running research project at the Etowah mounds with members of the Muscogee (Creek) Nation, the South Carolina Institute of Archaeology and Anthropology, and Texas State University, begun in 2005. Magnetometry and GPR have mapped a series of buildings on the summit of Mound A and clusters of buildings throughout the village. Since 2009, work with the Coosawattee Foundation has carried the mapping onto land around the site, with the goal of mapping as much of the surrounding landscape as possible.

The Etowah Archaeo-Geophysical Survey: Creating place and identity through the built environment. Journal of Archaeological Science: Reports 36 (2021). With Adam King and F. Kent Reilly.

Remote Sensing Data from Etowah's Mound A: Architecture and the Recreation of Mississippian Tradition. American Antiquity 76(2) (2011). With Adam King, Robert V. Sharp, F. Kent Reilly and Duncan McKinnon.

Bowie County, Texas

A cultural resource management project for a levee realignment. AGA located several Caddo buildings in two village areas, and a mound site linked to the Caddo community believed to be the Upper Nasoni village drawn on a 1692 Spanish expedition map.

A Revolution in Caddo Archaeology: The Remote Sensing and Archaeological View from the Hill Farm Site. Southeastern Archaeology 27(1) (2008). With Timothy K. Perttula and T. Clay Schultz.

Moundville, Alabama

Magnetometry across much of the Moundville site, used to study how the community there was laid out.

Remote Sensing as Community Settlement Analysis at Moundville. American Antiquity 80(1):161-169 (2015). With Jera Davis and John H. Blitz.

Mapoon earth mounds, Australia

GPR and magnetometry inside earth mounds at Mapoon, Cape York Peninsula, Queensland, combined with ethnohistory to understand how the mounds were built and used.

Integration of GPR and magnetics to study the interior features and history of earth mounds, Mapoon, Queensland, Australia. Archaeological Prospection 25(3) (2018). With Lawrence B. Conyers, Emma St. Pierre and Mary-Jean Sutton.

Reimagining life and death: Results and interpretations of geophysical and ethnohistorical investigations of earth mounds, Mapoon, Cape York Peninsula, Queensland, Australia. Archaeology in Oceania (2019). With Emma St. Pierre, Lawrence Conyers, Mary-Jean Sutton, Peter Mitchell and Dianne Nicholls.

Scorpion Mound, Mexico

An effigy mound complex in the Tehuacán Valley of Puebla.

The Scorpion Mound Complex: An Effigy Mound Site with Possible Functional/Ceremonial Significance in the Tehuacán Valley of Puebla, México. Ancient Mesoamerica (2025). With James Neely, Blas Castellón-Huerta, Samuel Wilson and Mark Willis.

Actuncan, Belize

Land holding at the ancient Maya site of Actuncan.

Land Tenure Systems at the Ancient Maya Site of Actuncan, Belize. Latin American Antiquity (2019). With Lisa J. LeCount, John H. Blitz and Ted C. Nelson.

Wari colonization of Nasca, Peru

Evidence for early Wari expansion into the Nasca region of southern Peru.

Early imperialism in the Andes: Wari colonization of Nasca. Antiquity 95(384):1527-1546 (2021). With Christina A. Conlee, Corina M. Kellner and Aldo Noriega.
